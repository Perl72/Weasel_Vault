What is in the data directory

  • accounts.tsv — the 28-set with posts, depth and capture hash.
  • edges_28.tsv — co-post edges where both ends are in the set.
  • nodes_28.tsv — partners and shared posts inside the set.
  • queue.txt, capped.txt — the 28 seeds, and the 15 that hit the scroll cap.
  • README.md — how it was made, counts, limits. SHA256SUMS covers the files.

Method

One account at a time with dl_wm's collabnet: scrape the timeline, write the word sort, move on. The per-scroll pause (3–6 s) is the rate limit; the account boundary is a short jittered pause, not a second one. Two empty or blocked accounts in a row widen the interval and are re-queued once; five in a row stop the run.

Two depths: a --sweep pass at 50 rounds (about 600 posts) and a --deep pass at 600 rounds for accounts that hit the cap. Snowballed collaborators go to a separate queue, so a pass finishes the set it was given.

Limits

Partial and mixed: one seed has no capture, the sweep captures hold about 600 posts each, and the rest go to 1,800. Edge counts are lower bounds and are not comparable across accounts. Live data changes between captures — these files are the snapshot. The whole-walk graph (1,600 accounts, 1,974 links) is not published here because it is not the 28-set.
